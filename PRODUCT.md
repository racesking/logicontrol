# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + three.js, chosen by the owner on 2026-11-06 for this design prototype. The Astro plan on `impeccable-design` does not apply to this branch.

## Users

The first reader is a private person, with residential and commercial work both in view (D21). In this phase the real audience is the owner, judging a design prototype. It is not yet a lead funnel.

## Product Purpose

Logicontrol lets a person monitor and command real-world processes: HVAC automation, BMS, hardware and software (D20). This branch is a from-scratch design prototype of its website. Success is a visual experience the owner accepts, not enquiries.

## Positioning

A person sees a real physical process and commands it. The command leaves, the equipment answers, and the measurement proves it happened. The firm builds the whole chain, from cabinet and wiring to the screen in front of the user.

## Operating Context

Work covers heating, ventilation, cooling, lighting, electrical cabinets, controllers and supervision screens, in houses and in buildings, across Romania. The firm programs Siemens and Schneider equipment. It is not an official partner of either.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Prototype only: no working enquiry, backend, CMS, analytics or hosting decision.
- Romanian only in this iteration (D24). English is required before any launch (D06).
- Every demonstration is simulated and must say so. No random numbers posing as live telemetry.
- The cabinet starts as pure three.js. A scripted Blender model is the agreed fallback if the owner is unhappy with it.
- Undecided: domain, trademark clearance, legal company name, contact details, enquiry destination, production host.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Logicontrol.
- Kept by the owner on 2026-11-06: Logo B, dark surfaces, the amber accent, engineering photography.
- Voice: plain, direct Romanian, with proper diacritics.
- Siemens and Schneider are named at most once, as equipment the firm programs. No partner claim and no logo wall (D23).
- Desigo Optic is a feel reference for the owner only. It is never named on the page, and its interface is not copied.
- The dashboard follows the structure of the owner's own BMS screens, kept outside Git. Only the look is reused: Logicontrol branding and an invented building, with no real project names, rooms, logos or values.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo files, Geist fonts and photographs live on `impeccable-design`, under `exploration/dark/`.
- The photographs are Unsplash stock (Immo Wegmann, Dean Brierley), not the firm's own work, and need credit.
- No named projects, clients, testimonials, certifications, staff or metrics exist. None may be invented. Real photos and details are available from the owner, but availability is not permission to publish (D09).

## Product Principles

1. Show the mechanism working instead of describing it.
2. Command and measurement stay distinct: a request is not a confirmation.
3. Never claim what cannot be proven. Label simulations and placeholders.
4. Treat real equipment with an engineer's accuracy, because that accuracy is the credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard access, visible focus and reduced-motion support. Every simulated control is a real HTML control, not canvas-only.
