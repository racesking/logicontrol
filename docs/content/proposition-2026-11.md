# Proposition brief
Accepted direction, 3 November 2026. Preview, not the production site.

Logicontrol helps someone with a home, or with an automation project still on paper, get fresher air without throwing the heat out of the window. Commercial buildings stay available, behind that story.

The visual system is dark Control. The mark is Logo B. Romanian leads. English is a separate page.

The demonstration is one winter morning in a bedroom: window shut, window tilted, or the principle of heat recovery. It shows heavy or fresher air and what happens to the heat. It does not switch a light, quote a saving, certify the air, or size a product.

The enquiry asks what is wrong, what the place is, and what equipment is already there. It must work even if the person never opens the demonstration. This preview does not send the form.

Do not claim a health result, an energy performance certificate, an energy audit, or a Casa Eficientă Energetic application. Household words on the first screen. KNX, BMS and setpoints stay off it.

Research behind the wording: [organic reach](../research/organic-reach-2026-11.md).
