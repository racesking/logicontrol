01 · Air in the home

Condensation and heavy air

Windows that keep heat in also keep moisture in. A bedroom shut all night can feel heavy in the morning, and condensation can form on the cold glass.

What people notice

Stale air, misted glass, sometimes mould by the frame. A CO₂ sensor does not certify the air and does not replace a doctor.

What we do not promise

We do not promise that mould, an allergy or a headache will go away. We can show what airing does to the air and to the heat, on an illustrative example.

02 · Heat and use

Airing that cools the house

A tilted window changes the air and lets the heat out. In the morning the boiler or heat pump warms cold air. Heat recovery is the principle by which fresh air comes in and most of the heat stays.

We do not publish a percentage cut in the bill. A saving figure appears only after an agreed measurement, for that home. A visit is not an energy audit and not an energy performance certificate.

    06 · Questions

    What we do not do, said plainly

    Does a tilted window fix condensation?

    It changes the air and can clear the glass, but it cools the room. That is why the demonstration shows the air and the heat together.

    Does air conditioning replace ventilation?

    A unit that recirculates the air already in the room does not, by itself, bring in outdoor air. It is not the same thing as ventilation.

    Can you use the boiler or heat pump already there?

    Yes. That is usually the starting point: what is already in the home. We do not assume everything is replaced.

    Do you issue an energy certificate or an energy audit?

    No. An energy performance certificate or an energy audit is signed by an attested auditor. A visit from us is not that document.

    Do you file a Casa Eficientă Energetic application?

    No. We do not enrol anyone in a funding programme. For an open session, check the official page at afm.ro.

    How far will the bill fall? Will the mould go away?

    We do not give a percentage and we do not promise a medical result. An illustrative example is not a measurement from your home.
