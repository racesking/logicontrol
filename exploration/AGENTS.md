# Identity exploration
Scope: exploration/ and descendants. Also follow root guidance.
Read BUILD_PLAN sections 5 and 8; use DECISIONS for owner preferences.

- Build three genuinely distinct concepts under light/, dark/ and mixed/. Use simple HTML/CSS/JS and original editable SVG.
- Each concept owns its directory; coordinator owns index.html. Do not change shared files from a concept task.
- Compare the same content, service/project cards and lighting mini-demo across concepts.
- Deliver two logo options plus monochrome treatment, semantic palette, contrast checks, licensed fonts with Romanian glyphs, responsive hero and demo.
- Use only labelled illustrative project cards until approved assets arrive.
- Good: distinctive typography/composition, useful motion, realistic control states, clear hierarchy.
- Bad: colour-swapped copies, generic glowing gradients, stock house/lightning symbols, copied logos, decorative fake telemetry.
- Label simulations; prototype contact actions must not claim successful enquiry submission.
- Test at 360/768/1440px, keyboard focus, reduced motion, links and overflow. Capture images or state lack of browser verification.
- Keep previews noindex. Do not scaffold the final app or choose the winning identity.
